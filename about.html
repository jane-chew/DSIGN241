About the Company

Our Mission

To help students connect with compatible study partners in a safe, supportive, and inclusive environment.

Our Vision

To create a university community where every student feels connected, supported, and empowered to achieve their academic goals.

Our Values

We believe learning is a team sport, not a solo race. We are here to lift each other up, share knowledge freely, and celebrate every win together. When one of us succeeds, we all do.

Our Purpose

Our purpose is to reduce barriers that prevent students from seeking academic support and building meaningful peer connections. Study Buddy aims to make finding study partners simple, comfortable, and effective.

Meet Our Team

Our Story

Created by a team of University students passionate about improving the way students connect and study together. Anika, Jane, Joelle and Paea collaborated to design a platform with inclusive services encouraging peers to find compatible study partners or groups, emphasising learning methods through an engaging, supportive and effective approach.

The Problem

Finding the right Study Partner isn’t Easy

Many students struggle to find study partners who share similar goals, learning styles, and availability. Existing social platforms and group chats often create mismatched expectations and unproductive sessions, making connections difficult for new students seeking supportive peers.

The Solution

A Smarter Way to Connect Students

Study Buddy helps students find compatible study partners based on subjects, study preferences, availability, and learning goals. By focusing on compatibility rather than random connections, the platform makes it easier to build productive and supportive study relationships.
